About Me

I am an Electrical & Computer Engineering Technology (ECET) student at the New Jersey Institute of Technology. My goal is to become an Electronics Engineer in the future. Before graduating college, I would like to gain industry experience through an internship or position as an Electronics Technician.

My broader technical interests include routing, switching, networking, radio communications, RF signals and systems, electrical troubleshooting, CAD design, PCB design, and field engineering. I enjoy learning through hands-on technical work involving electronics, hardware, and engineering design.

Outside of school and technology, my interests include making music, playing guitar, running, going to the gym, and playing video games.
